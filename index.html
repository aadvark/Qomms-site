Cast to Chromecast

Install the beta in three steps

  1. Download

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Say yes.

  3. Sign in

    Use your phone number. Telegram sends the code to your existing app.

Have an Android TV?

Qomms Receiver gives you more control than a Chromecast: a timeline and speed, audio and subtitle buttons on your remote, Up next, and formats a Chromecast can't play. Install it on the TV, then pick it in Qomms or type the code it shows.
