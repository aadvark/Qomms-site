Install in three steps.

  1. Download the app

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.

  2. Allow the install

    Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser for this file. Say yes when it does.

  3. Sign in

    Open Qomms and sign in with your phone number. Telegram sends the code to your existing app.

Have an Android TV?

Qomms Receiver runs on Android TV and Google TV, and can play formats a Chromecast can't, as long as your TV can decode them. Use it on your home Wi-Fi.
